What the listing says

Sun minutes are geometric: clear glass, no blinds, own roof and neighbouring buildings block the sun, trees are mostly not modelled, eave height is assumed. They say when direct sun can reach the floor, not how bright the room is. Exterior doors with unknown glazing are treated as transparent. No weather, diffuse light, furniture or blinds are simulated.
