Sun minutes are geometric: clear glass, no blinds, own roof and neighbouring buildings block the sun, trees are mostly not modelled, eave height is assumed. They say when direct sun can reach the floor, not how bright the room is.
Assumptions and precise definitionsAssumed / modelled

The same Python engine produces the yellow floor cells, room minutes and opening blocker verdicts. Daylight bins are five minutes, with a fractional final bin; the floor grid is 0.10 m and the qualifying patch is at least 0.1 m². Before / after noon is split at the engine’s solar-elevation maximum, located to one second.

All exterior doorways are included as conditionally fully transparent apertures. Their actual glazing is unknown. Window-only totals are shown on each room’s hover detail. Roof eaves, floor-to-ground height and some neighbour heights are assumed. Vegetation is mostly omitted; the Sara Rose house includes several estimated tree prisms and a hand-marked fitted porch edge. No clouds, diffuse light, blinds, frames, furniture or photometry are simulated.

The green rectangles project the scan’s measured openings into aerial photographs and panoramas. They can differ from visible trim or be hidden by an eave, foliage or blinds. Automated crop selection outside the hand-marked example is not an independently checked window correspondence.
